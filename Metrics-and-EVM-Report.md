## Planned Value (PV)

The project consists of three sprints. By the end of Sprint 2, 2/3 of the project work is completed.

**Formula**

PV = Total Story Points × (2/3)

**Calculation**

PV = 93 × (2/3)

PV = **62**

---

## Earned Value (EV)

Earned Value represents the amount of work actually completed in Sprint 2.

**Formula**

EV = Sprint 1 Completed + Sprint 2 Completed

**Calculation**

EV = 30 + 37

EV = **67**

---

## Actual Cost (AC)

Actual Cost is assumed to equal 2/3 of total project effort by the end of Sprint 2.

AC = **62**

---

## Schedule Performance Index (SPI)

**Formula**

SPI = EV ÷ PV

**Calculation**

SPI = 67 ÷ 62

SPI = **1.08**

### Interpretation

An SPI greater than 1.0 indicates the project is ahead of schedule.

---

## Cost Performance Index (CPI)

**Formula**

CPI = EV ÷ AC

**Calculation**

CPI = 67 ÷ 62

CPI = **1.08**

### Interpretation

A CPI greater than 1.0 shows that the project is performing better than planned.

---

## Estimate at Completion (EAC)

Budget at Completion (BAC) = 93 Story Points

**Formula**

EAC = BAC ÷ CPI

**Calculation**

EAC = 93 ÷ 1.08

EAC = **86.1**

---

## Estimate to Complete (ETC)

**Formula**

ETC = EAC − EV

**Calculation**

ETC = 86.1 − 67

ETC = **19.1**
