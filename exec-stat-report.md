# Executive Status Report: Patient Management Platform

**Overall health: Green, ahead of schedule and under budget.** After Sprint 2 of 3, the team has delivered 67 of 93 story points (72%) against a plan of 62, and is forecast to finish on time with about 7% of the effort budget to spare.

## Milestones

| Release | Business value delivered | Points | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint 1: Digital intake and scheduling | Paperless patient and consent forms, online scheduling, automated reminders, secure login | 30 | Complete |
| Sprint 2: Insurance and identity verification | Insurance pre-checks, ID and multi-step identity verification, document uploads and expiration alerts | 37 | Complete |
| Sprint 3: Automation and billing | Automated billing and payment reminders, AI call handling, waitlist auto-fill, patient activity log | 26 | Next |

Team output rose 23% from Sprint 1 to Sprint 2 (30 to 37 points). Sprint 3 needs 26, less than either sprint so far.

## Schedule and cost performance

Both indexes are 1.08: for every point of work planned, the team is delivering about 1.08.

| Cost Performance Index (CPI) | 1.08 | 8% more work per unit of effort than planned |
| Schedule variance (EV − PV) | +5 points | 67 delivered vs. 62 planned |
| Cost variance (EV − AC) | +5 points | 67 delivered for 62 spent |
| Estimate at completion (EAC) | 86.1 points | vs. 93-point budget |

There are no unfavorable variances to report. All figures are in story points; actual cost is estimated at the planned rate, not tracked separately.

## Top risks

| Risk | Business impact | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI call system is the most complex remaining feature (8 points) | Could delay the final release | Start it first in Sprint 3; ship the rest if it slips |
| Billing and payment automation handles patient financial data | Errors would affect revenue and patient trust | Test billing and payment reminders end to end before launch |
| Actual cost is estimated, not measured | The cost picture may be more favorable than reality | Track actual hours in Sprint 3 to confirm CPI |

## Next steps

1. **Deliver Sprint 3** (26 points), finishing automation and billing so the platform is complete end to end.
2. **Prioritize the AI call system** early in the sprint to protect the completion date.
3. **Start tracking actual effort** so final cost reporting rests on measured data.
4. **Plan the launch:** clinic staff onboarding and rollout once Sprint 3 is accepted.

**Decisions needed from leadership:** none at this time. The project is on track within its approved scope and budget.
